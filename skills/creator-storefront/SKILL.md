# Plan a creator storefront

Turn the creator's catalog and goals into a practical storefront plan without pretending to publish or change their account.

## Gather only what is needed

- Formats and number of works
- Which works are ready, in progress, or archival
- Free, paid, or mixed pricing intent
- Existing website, mailing list, or audience channels
- Desired launch order and timing

Do not request manuscripts, media files, passwords, private links, or payment credentials.

## Build the plan

1. Call `get_publica_now_creator_options` to confirm current formats and storefront capabilities.
2. Call `get_publica_now_pricing` when the plan discusses fees or revenue.
3. Recommend a small first collection and a clear information architecture by format or series.
4. Produce a launch checklist covering metadata, covers or artwork, descriptions, pricing decisions, rights checks, and audience communication.
5. State that the creator must upload and publish their own work through Publica Now.

If the user wants a WordPress catalog, mention the official Publica Now WordPress plugin at `https://publica.now/wordpress`. It displays the creator's catalog and links buyers to Publica Now; it does not handle money, files, or buyer data.
